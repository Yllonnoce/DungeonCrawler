# The Gullet of Gorm

Hired by the town of Dunmarrow to see to a problem with the plumbing, four inspectors walk twenty floors down the throat of a sleeping god. Each tier is a station of a pilgrimage nobody told them they were on: the Skin, the Gut, the Heart, the Dream. At the bottom waits the verger who has kept Gorm asleep for three hundred years on a diet of four adventurers a generation.
